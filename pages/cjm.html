Customer Intelligence Engine

Architecting a unified source of truth for customer journey data - from zero budget to an AI-powered knowledge ecosystem.

Tl;Dr

As the first person to tackle this problem at TET, I identified that customer journey data was fragmented across Marketing, Sales, and Support - with no shared language, no central hub, and no budget. I designed and deployed a 4-tier knowledge architecture (the "Intelligence Pyramid"), integrated a cross-linked toolkit across Figjam, Figma, and XWiki, and pioneered three custom AI agents on our internal LLM infrastructure to automate insight generation at scale.

The Story

The Problem: Three Teams, Zero Shared Truth

Marketing tracked acquisition funnels. Sales tracked pipeline stages. Support tracked ticket categories. None of it talked to each other. As a result, decisions were made on partial pictures - and the customer experience suffered for it.

I was brought in as the pioneer for this initiative at TET, tasked with establishing a framework that could consolidate insights and enable cross-departmental decision-making. The constraint: zero dedicated budget.

The core question I started with: If a customer calls support today, do we know what journey they've been on - and why they're frustrated?

The Strategy: The Intelligence Pyramid

Rather than trying to fix everything at once, I designed a hierarchical information architecture - four tiers, each feeding the one above it, moving from raw evidence up to strategic clarity.

Level 1 · Strategic Master lifecycle maps - end-to-end customer journey visibility
Level 2 · Analytical Segmented journey maps - pain points and friction by persona
Level 3 · Operational Service blueprints - internal systems mapped to touchpoints
Level 4 · Evidence Centralized library - raw data, research, and technical artifacts

The Implementation: A Cross-Linked Toolkit

I integrated three tools that the teams were already using - Figjam for visual strategy, Figma for UX artifacts, and XWiki as the operational knowledge base - into a cohesive, searchable ecosystem. The key was making them talk to each other.

  • Cross-linking strategy: Visual journey maps in Figjam were directly linked to technical process flows and research evidence in XWiki, so any stakeholder could trace from "user insight" down to "system log" without asking anyone.
  • Standardization: I created templates, naming conventions, and governance rules to keep the data consistent, clean, and ready for future AI ingestion.
  • Repository syncs: Regular cross-team sessions ensured that UX research findings were continuously fed back into the operational blueprints - closing the loop between discovery and implementation.

The Technical Edge: AI Agents at Scale

Once the framework was in place and the data was structured, I pioneered the use of custom AI agents on TET's internal LLM infrastructure to remove the manual bottleneck of analysis. I designed three specialized agents, each with a distinct role:

🗺️

Journey Mapping Agent

Generates end-to-end journey maps from unstructured customer feedback automatically, with standardized visualization across all segments.

🔍

Pain Point Discovery Agent

Performs pattern recognition across support logs and feedback to surface friction points before they become systemic - proactive, not reactive.

⚙️

Data Synthesis Agent

Processes disparate data streams from .xlsx reports and maps quantitative inputs directly to qualitative journey stages.
